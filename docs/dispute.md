# Disputes

## MERCHANDISE_OR_SERVICE_NOT_RECEIVED
**Buyer claims:** the item never arrived.

**How to win:** the seller proves delivery.
- Tracking shows **delivered** to the address on the PayPal transaction
- Shipping and delivery dates match the seller's timeline
- The seller's statement matches the tracking history

**Example:** Buyer says it never arrived. Seller shows tracking delivered on Oct 15 to the PayPal address, shipped Oct 13 as stated.

## MERCHANDISE_OR_SERVICE_NOT_AS_DESCRIBED
**Buyer claims:** the item is wrong or damaged.

**How to win:** show the item matched the listing.
- Listing description and photos
- Photos of the item before shipping
- Buyer's proof doesn't match the claim

**Example:** Buyer says it's damaged; seller has pre-shipment photos showing it intact and packed.

## UNAUTHORISED
**Buyer claims:** they didn't make the purchase (fraud).

**How to win:** show the purchase came from the real account holder, or that the item went where PayPal said to send it.
- Delivered to the address on the PayPal transaction (required)
- Signature confirmation if $750+ (required)
- Earlier undisputed orders from the same account
- Buyer contacted the seller about the order
- Seller Shield risk score was LOW at checkout
